Welcome

Welcome to the Newport & District Air Rifle League, a competitive shooting league bringing together teams from across South Wales. We're passionate about promoting marksmanship, sportsmanship, and camaraderie through the sport of air rifle shooting.

Whether you're an experienced shooter looking to join a team or someone interested in learning more about our league, we invite you to explore our site and get in touch.
