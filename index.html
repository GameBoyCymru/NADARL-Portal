Newport & District Air Rifle League

Comprising 5 Teams Shooting 7 Yard Bell Target Weekly

Welcome

Newport & District Air Rifle League is a competitive air rifle shooting league, whose members compete in team matches on Monday evenings throughout the season. Each team can field up to 9 shooters, with the top scores counting towards the match result. We also host competitions open to all members, and an annual Open competition where everyone is welcome.

We hold open evenings at regular intervals throughout the year so that non-members can come along and try our sport.

All events use .177 calibre air rifles with open dioptre sights, firing at a painted metal bell target at 7 yards. A clean bull scores 10 points, graduating down to 0. Field target rifles are permitted if power is reduced below 9.26 joules or 6.83 ft/lbs.
