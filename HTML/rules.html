Newport & District Air Rifle League

Rules & Regulations

Equipment

  • Calibre: All shooting is conducted with .177 calibre (4.5 mm) air rifles only.
  • Power limit: Rifle muzzle energy must not exceed 9.26 joules (6.83 ft/lbs). Any rifle may be chronographed at the discretion of the Match Secretary.
  • Pellets: Only standard lead or lead-alloy airgun pellets of .177 calibre are permitted. No explosive, tracer or incendiary ammunition is allowed.
  • Sights: Open or dioptre iron sights only. Telescopic, red-dot, laser or any optical sighting aid is prohibited.
  • Field target rifles: Permitted provided the power is reduced below 9.26 joules (6.83 ft/lbs) and iron sights are fitted.
  • Trigger: Triggers must be safe and predictable. The Match Secretary may bar any rifle deemed unsafe.

The Range & Target

  • Distance: All matches are shot at a fixed distance of 7 yards.
  • Target: A painted steel bell target is used. The striker rings the bell on a successful strike.
  • Scoring ring: The target face is marked with concentric scoring rings. A clean bull scores 10 points, graduating down to 0 for a complete miss.
  • Shot count: Each shooter fires a set string of shots per match (typically seven counting shots).

Match Conduct

  • Team size: Each team may field up to 9 shooters per match, with the top scores counting towards the match result.
  • League season: Matches are shot on Monday evenings throughout the season as published in the fixture list.
  • Scoring: Match results are decided on aggregate team score. A clean bull counts 10, with scores graduating downward by ring.
  • Shooter conduct: Shooters must remain quiet and still on the firing point while others are shooting. Coaching from the firing point is not permitted during a counting string.
  • Safety: Rifles must be kept unloaded, actions open and pointed in a safe direction except when on the firing point. The Range Officer's word is final on all safety matters.
  • Disputes: Any dispute over a score or conduct must be referred to the Match Secretary, whose decision is final on the day.

Competitions

In addition to the weekly league, the NADARL hosts open competitions during the season and an annual Open competition where non-members are welcome to enter. The equipment and conduct rules above apply to all competitions unless the competition entry sheet states otherwise.
