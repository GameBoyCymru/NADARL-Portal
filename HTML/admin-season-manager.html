Seasons

Manage seasons. Matches, Exceptions, Competitions and Events each have their own section below.

Set as Current controls which season pages like Fixtures, League Table and Summer League show by default - pick the selected season above and click it any time, not just when a season is first created. Move Up/Down reorders the season list - use it to slot a newly-added historical season in before others, or fix one entered out of order. Reset Season clears all scores and stats for the selected season, including any Personal Best set or manually entered within it - a shooter's all-time Personal Best is just the highest Season Best across every season. Other seasons are untouched.
No season yet? Add one (e.g. "2026-27"). New seasons are added to the end of the list - use Move Up/Down above to reposition, e.g. to backfill an older season before the current one:

Match Days Required

How many match days it takes for every team to play every other team both home and away, in the League half and again in the Handicap half.

Match Days Allocated

How many distinct match days already have fixtures scheduled in the season selected above, in the League half and the Handicap half.

Matches

Manually schedule matches for the selected season, then correct an existing fixture's date or venue below (postponements, venue changes). Adding a match checks the day for you: a team already scheduled that day is blocked outright, and a venue already in use that day (teams sharing a ground) asks for confirmation before going ahead. Editing venue on an existing match is a permanent override - it will no longer follow the home team's registered venue if that changes later. Leave venue blank to follow the home team's venue.

Pick the date once, then add every match being played that day below.

Exceptions

Mark a Monday as having no league matches (bank holidays, cup nights, catch-up weeks). Exceptions show on the public Fixtures page as a blocked day. Adding, moving or removing one only shifts fixtures that are actually in the way - a week later to make room, or a week earlier to close the gap - stopping as soon as it reaches a free week.
