Newport & District Air Rifle League

A Legacy of Marksmanship

Founded over a century ago, the Newport & District Air Rifle League has a rich and proud history. From humble beginnings in local clubrooms to becoming one of the most respected shooting leagues in South Wales, our story is one of dedication, sportsmanship, and community.

Through the Years

1907

The League is Founded

A group of passionate marksmen from across the Newport area came together to form the Newport & District Air Rifle League. Meetings were held in church halls and community centres, with competitors using basic spring-powered air rifles. The founding clubs recognised a shared desire for friendly competition and structured shooting events.

1920s

Growth and Expansion

Following the First World War, interest in target shooting surged as returning servicemen sought to continue their marksmanship skills. The league expanded to include clubs from surrounding valleys and towns, establishing a formal fixture calendar and introducing the first league championship trophy.

1950s

Post-War Revival

After a period of suspension during the Second World War, the league was revitalised with renewed enthusiasm. New clubs joined the ranks, and the introduction of improved air rifle technology brought higher scores and more competitive matches. The annual presentation evening became a highlight of the sporting calendar.

1970s

Modernisation Era

The league embraced modernisation with the construction of dedicated indoor shooting ranges across several member clubs. Standardised scoring systems were introduced, and the league established formal rules and governance structures that would shape its operation for decades to come.

2000s

A New Millennium

The turn of the century saw the league continue to thrive with a new generation of shooters taking up the sport. Junior development programmes were launched to nurture young talent, and the league celebrated its longstanding traditions while looking ahead to a bright future in competitive air rifle shooting.

Present Day

The League Today

Today the Newport & District Air Rifle League continues to uphold its proud heritage. With member clubs fielding teams across multiple divisions, the league remains a cornerstone of the South Wales shooting community, promoting fair competition, camaraderie, and the highest standards of marksmanship.
