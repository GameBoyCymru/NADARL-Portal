Newport & District Air Rifle League

A Legacy of Marksmanship

The Newport & District Air Rifle League is a traditional bell target league and 10 metre air rifle club based in Newport, South Wales, with roots going back to 1907. We welcome shooters of all ages and experience levels, and equipment is available for those still building up their own kit.
